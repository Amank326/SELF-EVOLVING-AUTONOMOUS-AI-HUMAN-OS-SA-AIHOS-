## 💡 **Pro Tips**

### Tip 1: The Demo is Your Strongest Asset
Practice it. Make it smooth. The 3D animation is visually impressive, so let it speak for itself. Your job is to narrate what's happening, not over-explain.

### Tip 2: Adapt Your Language to Your Audience
- **Engineers:** Talk about architecture, latency, optimizations
- **Business/Investors:** Talk about markets, use cases, problems solved
- **Recruiters:** Talk about what you built and what you learned
- **Researchers:** Talk about research questions and future directions

Use USE_CASES.md and INTERVIEW_PREP.md for audience-specific language.

### Tip 3: Always Link Back to the Loop
Whatever you're explaining, connect it to: **Think → Act → Reflect → Evolve**

This is the heart of the system. Everything else is details.

### Tip 4: Admit Uncertainty
If someone asks about something you haven't thought through, say:
> "That's a great question. [Current state]. The research question is: [what we'd need to validate]."

This is intellectually honest and shows you've thought deeply.

### Tip 5: Have Evidence Ready
Every major claim should be backed by one of the documents:
- "It's novel" → SYSTEM_OVERVIEW.md Section 5
- "It actually learns" → RESEARCH_NOTES.md Section 1.5 or EvolutionEngine.kt
- "It's fast" → DEMO_GUIDE.md or ARCHITECTURE.md
- "It matters" → USE_CASES.md

If someone challenges you, say: "The evidence is in [document]."
