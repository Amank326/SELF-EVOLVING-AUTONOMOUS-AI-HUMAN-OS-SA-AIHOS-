## ✨ PROJECT HIGHLIGHTS

### 5-Layer AI Architecture
1. **Memory Layer**: Store episodes, facts, rules, attention
2. **Reasoning Engine**: Generate options, score decisions
3. **Reflection Layer**: Analyze outcomes, extract insights
4. **Evolution Engine**: Update rules, create new ones
5. **Autonomy Controller**: Full decision loop orchestration

### MVVM Android Architecture
- **ViewModels**: StateFlow-based reactive state
- **Compose UI**: 4 screens with Material3 design
- **Database**: Room with 5 entities
- **DI**: Hilt for dependency injection
- **Async**: Coroutines for background operations

### Safety & Transparency
- 5 autonomy levels (DISABLED → FULL_AUTONOMOUS)
- Rate limiting and constraint enforcement
- Complete decision audit trail
- Explainable reasoning for every choice
