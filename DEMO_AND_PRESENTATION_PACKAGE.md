## 💡 The Core Story (Memorize This)

### 30 Seconds
> "SA-AIHOS is a self-evolving AI that makes its thinking visible through real-time 3D animation. Unlike ChatGPT, it runs on your phone, learns from interactions, and you can influence its reasoning through touch. It demonstrates a new approach: observable, trustworthy AI."

### 2 Minutes
> "SA-AIHOS is an autonomous learning system with a unique property: its reasoning is observable. Most AI hides what it's thinking—you get outputs without understanding why. SA-AIHOS runs a continuous loop: think (generate options), act (execute), reflect (analyze outcome), evolve (update decision weights). The entire process is visualized in real-time through 3D animation that changes color, animation speed, and particle effects based on what the AI is actually thinking.
>
> Technically, it's Kotlin for reasoning + JavaScript for visualization. Sub-100ms latency, fits in <50MB memory, runs at 60 FPS on mobile.
>
> Why it matters: This enables three things. First, safety—medical teams can validate AI intent. Second, learning—the AI improves from interaction. Third, trust—if you watch it think, you understand it. Different category entirely from stateless chatbots."

[1:40-2:20] LEARNING
"It learns. Each interaction is a decision. Reflects on outcome. Updates weights."

[2:20-3:00] CLOSING
